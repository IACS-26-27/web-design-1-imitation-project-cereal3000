30 Percent Solution

How much carbon pollution could New England eliminate by fully leveraging the power of its forests? A lot: 30 percent of the CO2 emmisions reductions we need to meet the region's net-zero goals.
We've run the numbers on four climate solutions:
  • Stop net loss of forests, including both wildlands and working woodlands
  • Spread use of our Exemplary Forestry and other climate-smart forestry
  • Use renewable building materials to replace polluting steel and concrete
  • Lock up carbon for life in strong wood buildings
Combined, these approaches could provide nearly 1/3 of the reduction in energy-related CO2 emissions—or, emissions from fossil-fuel combustion—we need by 2050, with most savings coming from climate-smart forestry.
